What is HRA Exemption Calculator? Section 10(13A) Guide 2026

HRA (House Rent Allowance) is allowance given by employer for rent. As per Section 10(13A) of Income Tax Act, HRA is partially tax-free if you live on rent. Our Rently HRA Exemption Calculator calculates exact tax-free HRA as per government 3 rules. It is 100% free, unlimited, no watermark, works worldwide for India. HRA exemption is only available in Old Tax Regime, not in New Regime 2026 where HRA is fully taxable.

Example: Basic Rs 6L yearly, HRA received Rs 3L yearly, Rent paid Rs 25k monthly = Rs 3L yearly, Metro city. Rule 1: Actual HRA = 3L. Rule 2: Rent -10% Basic = 3L -60k = 2.4L. Rule 3: 50% Basic = 3L. Minimum is 2.4L, so exemption = Rs 2.4L tax-free, remaining Rs 60k taxable. If tax slab 20%, tax saved = 2.4L*20% = Rs 48k yearly, Rs 4k monthly.

How HRA Calculator Works? 3 Rules Formula

As per Income Tax Act, HRA exemption is MINIMUM of these 3: 1) Actual HRA received from employer. 2) Rent paid minus 10% of (Basic+DA). Annual rent -10% basic. 3) 50% of Basic+DA if living in metro (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), 40% if non-metro. Our calculator calculates all 3 and shows minimum as exemption. Remaining HRA is taxable and added to income.

Rent Calculation: If monthly rent Rs 25k, annual rent = 3L. 10% basic = 60k (6L*10%). So rent -10% basic = 2.4L. If you increase rent to Rs 30k monthly (3.6L yearly), then rent -10% basic = 3L, then exemption becomes 3L full. So paying higher rent increases exemption till limit of HRA received or 50% basic whichever lower.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How much HRA is tax-free?
HRA tax-free is minimum of 3: Actual HRA, Rent-10% Basic, 50% Basic metro / 40% non-metro. For 6L basic, 3L HRA, 25k rent metro, exemption = 2.4L.
Q2. Is HRA exempt in new tax regime?
No, HRA exemption is NOT available in New Regime 2026. It is only available in Old Regime with deductions. In new regime HRA fully taxable.
Q3. Can I claim HRA if I live with parents?
Yes, if you pay rent to parents with genuine rent agreement and bank transfer, you can claim HRA. Parents will show rental income and pay tax.
Q4. What is metro city for HRA?
Metro for HRA = Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai only. 50% of basic exemption. All other cities are non-metro with 40% basic.
